# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person on the other side is trying to do good work.
Criticise code, not people.

## Standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Giving and accepting constructive feedback with specifics rather than verdicts.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and fixing them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it.
- Being welcoming to people whose background, experience or first language
  differs from yours.

Behaviour that does not belong here:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or review.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages — and anywhere someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by contacting the maintainer ([@timzifer](https://github.com/timzifer))
directly on GitHub. Reports are handled privately, and whoever reports a
problem is not obliged to have handled it themselves first.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this document, and
may temporarily or permanently block anyone whose behaviour they judge
inappropriate. Where the response is more than a single removed comment, the
reasoning will be communicated to the person concerned.
